Journal entry #01. Velvet catalyst
(Red Velvet infused with Cream Cheese)

When I learned of Baron Baskerville's malicious intent and his meticulous plan to plunge the city into darkness, I knew I had to act without delay. His mole army possesses an innate ability to see through the darkest of environments, granting them a formidable advantage beneath the shadows. To counter this, I turned to the one thing I could trust above all else: my feline mind and the precision of my laboratory.

After countless trials, meticulous calibrations, and a few rather questionable hours of sleep, I successfully formulated a compound designed to enhance the senses—particularly vision. Thus, the Velvet catalyst was born.

The Velvet catalyst is a chemically balanced crimson base infused with the rich essence of a molten cream cheese core; this exquisite confection is more than a delectable palate experience, but rather, a power-up that goes beyond human limitations. A formula crafted not merely for indulgence, but to see what others cannot.


Sincerely,

The Professor
