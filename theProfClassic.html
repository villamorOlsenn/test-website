Journal Entry #02. The Professor’s Classic
(Classic Chocolate with Himalayan salt)

Baron Baskerville has grown increasingly bold. His offenses have become more calculated, his strength more formidable. I fear that simple evasion will no longer suffice. If my labbies are to withstand his assaults, I must develop a formula that does not merely strengthen the body—but fortifies it.

After countless trials, I have finally perfected it. The Professor’s Classic: A rich chocolate cookie engineered around a concentrated chocolatey core, designed to deliver a remarkable increase in muscular density upon consumption. The formula reinforces the body’s structural strength, allowing its bearer to endure impacts that would otherwise prove overwhelming.

Yet strength without balance is merely brute force. Thus, I introduced a precise measure of Himalayan salt—a mineral refinement intended to temper the richness of the chocolate while completing the formula’s delicate equilibrium. The result is deceptively simple: a dark, indulgent exterior concealing a molten chocolate center, carrying within it a formula capable of turning the ordinary into something considerably more resilient.

I have named it after myself for one reason: Some formulas are too important to remain unnamed.


Sincerely,

The Professor
